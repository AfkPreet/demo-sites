The game · Spring 2027

Lumen & the Lantern Rot

Every hundred years the woods forget how to hold light.

Lumen is nine, four foot one, and the only lamplighter left who still fits through the small doors. She has one autumn to relight the ninety-nine lamps of Bramblehollow before the Rot finishes eating them — and a lantern that runs on courage, and, when the courage runs out, on moss.

Nothing in these woods wants to hurt her. That's rather the trouble. The badger wants to talk. The toad wants paying. The dark just wants to be somewhere else, and keeps choosing here.

  • One lantern, four jobs. A light, a key, a lever and a friend — never a weapon.
  • Ninety-nine lamps. No two are lit the same way, and eleven of them argue.
  • Nothing to fight. Everything in the woods can be talked to, fed, or out-waited.
  • Drawn on paper. Twelve frames a second, painted in gouache, scanned, shaken slightly.

The studio

Three people and a kettle

Mossfoot started in 2019 as a game jam entry that nobody finished on time.

We rent the room above Halfpenny Cycles on Gloucester Road. It smells faintly of inner tubes. There are three desks, one very loud kettle, and a rule that anything we can draw by hand, we draw by hand — because the wobble in a scanned line is the whole reason the woods feel alive.

We ship slowly and we answer every email. If you are a publisher, a player, or someone who once made a game and stopped, we would genuinely like to hear from you.

Devlog · every other Friday

What we broke recently

  1. No. 41

    The toad problem

    12 August 2026

    Gob was too helpful. Playtesters treated the toad as a hint button and stopped looking at the woods entirely, so now Gob charges for advice — in acorns, which are useless, which is the joke.

  2. No. 40

    Rain that lands on things

    28 July 2026

    Six weeks on a rain shader nobody asked for. It puddles in the cart ruts and it dims the lantern by exactly eleven percent. Worth it.

  3. No. 39

    We deleted the map

    9 July 2026

    Bramblehollow now remembers where you have been by lighting it. The map is the lamps. Everyone got lost for a week and then nobody did.

  4. No. 38

    Pip learned to be bored

    21 June 2026

    If you stand still for thirty seconds they sit down. At sixty they fall asleep. Half the studio's productivity went into watching them do it.
