01 Method

Everything here is lit by the same light.

That is the whole method. One decision made early — where the light comes from — and then a hundred small ones that agree with it. Which edge catches, which one falls away, what casts a shadow onto what.

It is not a style. It is the reason a page feels made rather than assembled, and it is the only thing I know of that a template cannot fake.

03 Studio

One person, start to finish.

I design and build. There is no handoff, no spec document that loses half its intent on the way to a developer, and no second invoice for making it move. The six sites above were art-directed, typeset, written and shipped by me — the shaders included.

The work sits in the narrow band where art direction meets engineering: real-time 3D, scroll choreography, type systems, and the deeply unglamorous performance work that makes all of it feel effortless on a four-year-old Android.

Available for new work now, booking two to four weeks out. Based in Bengaluru, working across time zones.
